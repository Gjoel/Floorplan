Heights & roof

Floor levels are each room's height relative to the building's floor height — negative steps down. Stairs and entry steps read the levels at their two ends and set their own rise and treads. The roof, walls and levels all show in the 3D view.

Measure

Tick Print on a tape to carry it into the export — unticked tapes stay on screen only. Backspace takes the last one off while measuring; click a row to frame it.

Setback lines

Use these for a setback that has no boundary edge of its own — a road reserve, an easement, a neighbouring dwelling, the face of the next home. Drawn areas (decks, alfrescos, paved bays) can be the target as well as the building.

Boundary by bearings and distances
One line per boundary line, in order around the block: bearing, distance. Bearings read clockwise from north and follow the north angle set in Plot settings. Write them as 92°15'30", 92 15 30 or 92.2583. Lines starting with # are ignored. Leave off the last line and the block closes itself.
